## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added

- Typed Fable bindings for JavaScript Temporal, including instants, zoned date-times, calendar dates, wall-clock times, and durations.
- Typed construction, arithmetic, rounding, calendar, and time-zone options with nanosecond precision.
- Result-returning operations, error classification, and F# comparison helpers.
- Support for application-supplied Temporal implementations and native Temporal.
